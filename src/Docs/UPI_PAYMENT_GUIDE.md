# UPI Payment Guide for Sathiyan Sports

## How UPI Payment Works

### Option 1: Mobile Devices
1. **Click "Pay with UPI/Google Pay" button** - This will try to open your payment app directly
2. **Complete the payment** in your UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
3. **Copy the transaction ID** from your payment app
4. **Go to "Manual Entry" tab** and enter the transaction ID

### Option 2: QR Code (Recommended)
1. **Scan the QR code** shown in the payment dialog using any UPI app
2. **Complete the payment** in your UPI app
3. **Copy the transaction ID** from your payment app
4. **Go to "Manual Entry" tab** and enter the transaction ID

### Option 3: Manual Payment
1. **Copy the UPI ID**: smartsatheesh7-1@okhdfcbank
2. **Open any UPI app** (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
3. **Send money** to the UPI ID with the exact amount shown
4. **Add note**: "Booking payment for [Sport Name]"
5. **Copy the transaction ID** after successful payment
6. **Go to "Manual Entry" tab** and enter the transaction ID

## Important Notes

- **Payment Timer**: You have 5 minutes to complete the payment
- **Transaction ID**: This is crucial for confirming your payment
- **Amount**: Pay the exact amount shown in the booking
- **UPI ID**: Always use `smartsatheesh7-1@okhdfcbank`

## Troubleshooting

### If UPI App Doesn't Open
- **Use QR Code**: Scan with your camera or UPI app
- **Manual Payment**: Use the UPI ID directly in your app
- **Copy UPI ID**: Use the "Copy" button for easy pasting

### If Payment Fails
- **Check Amount**: Ensure you're paying the exact amount
- **Check UPI ID**: Verify the UPI ID is correct
- **Try Again**: Cancel and create a new booking if needed

### Transaction ID Issues
- **Find Transaction ID**: Look in your payment app under transaction history
- **Format**: Usually starts with numbers or letters (e.g., "T12345678" or "UPI123456")
- **Case Sensitive**: Enter exactly as shown in your payment app
